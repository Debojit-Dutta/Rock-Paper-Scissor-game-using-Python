Rock Paper Scissor Game 
A simple game built using python. Player enters a choice and the computer randomly choice rock, paper, scissor and then determine who is the winner. 
Technology used:
1.python 
2.Random module.
How to play:
when the program starts enter r for rock, p for paper and s for scissor. Then computer will show his choice and show win lose or draw.
