#Rock Paper Scissor Game 


A simple game built using python. Player enters a choice and the computer randomly choice rock, paper, scissor and then determine who is the winner. 




#Technology used:


1.python 


2.Random module.



#How to play:


when the program starts, enter 


1.'r' for rock


2.'p' for paper and 


3.'s' for scissor


Then computer will show his choice and show win lose or draw.
